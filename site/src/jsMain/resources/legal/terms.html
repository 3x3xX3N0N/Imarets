Terms (stub)

NEEDS COUNSEL BEFORE PAYMENTS OPEN.

This is a plain-language outline of the terms verdantbloom.bar intends to open with. It is not a contract. Nothing is for sale on this site, no payment is taken, and no account exists yet. It is published so you can see the rules before there is anything to agree to.

As of 2026-09-20.

1. What this is

verdantbloom.bar is a business. It will sell metered access to an OpenAI-compatible API in front of a lineup of open-weight language models, sold as a fiction-writing and developer API. It is for people 18 or older. The acceptable-use policy is part of these terms.

2. Today

This site has a tab signup and a reduced-fee application form. Neither charges anything yet. What they store is set out in Privacy.

3. Passes and the tab

  • A pass is a monthly tab in dollars, spent at the per-token rates on the pour list. It has a bottom: when the tab is dry, buy another round or wait for the reset.
  • Prices are the whole mandatory price, tax excluded.
  • The tab resets every 30 days. Unused tab does not roll over.
  • Context is billed as input on every request. There is no cached-input discount.
  • Reasoning tokens from thinking models are billed as output tokens, including reasoning your client hides.
  • Every pour has a small minimum, shown per shelf. A cold pour has a larger one.
  • Each pass has request limits: a number per minute and a number at the same time.
  • Taps are shared. Each tap is one GPU running one model; a few members may be served by the same tap at the same time.
  • Cold pours take about 30 seconds because taps scale to zero when the bar is quiet. A tap goes cold 60 seconds after your last request. No speed or availability level is promised.

4. Renewal and cancelling

Passes renew monthly until cancelled. Cancel any time from your account; the pass runs to the end of the paid period.

5. Another round

Rounds are dollar for dollar onto your tab. They need an active pass, are poured only after the monthly tab is dry, do not reset with the tab, and expire 12 months after purchase. No bonus credit, no bulk discount.

6. Prices move

Prices are our best estimate of cost, not an introductory offer. They may go up or down, with notice. The published volume schedule lowers per-token rates as the count of paid members holds above each line; its rules (two months to take effect, held at least six months, stepped back only after three months below the line and 30 days notice) are on the prices page. Pass prices stay put under the schedule. Minimum pours do not change.

7. Reduced-fee seats

A separate, application-based programme funded by patrons, not a sale. Eligibility is financial only, on the honour system. A seat is approved for a 6-month term and its funding is reserved for the whole term at approval. Seats are limited by the pool balance and capped at 25% of active passes (with a floor of 3 seats while there are fewer than 12 members). We email 14 days before the term ends. At term end the pass does not roll to the standard price by itself: it stops renewing unless you re-apply or choose the standard price. No price ever rises without your click.

8. A round for the house

A round for the house is a voluntary payment to a business, earmarked by us for reduced-fee seats. It is not a donation to a nonprofit and is not tax-deductible. It buys the patron no tokens, no priority and no rate change. Every dollar goes into the house pool; the house pays the card processor's fee itself. The pool pays verdantbloom the gap between what a reduced-fee member pays and the standard pass price, and nothing else. Unpoured rounds are refundable on request within 30 days. If the programme ever closes, the remaining pool balance is refunded to patrons pro rata to their rounds in the trailing 12 months. It never becomes house revenue. The ledger is published monthly.

9. Fair use of the taps

A tap costs money by the minute it is warm. We measure the GPU time each key holds. A key whose measured GPU cost in a period runs far above what it has been charged (the working figure is 1.5 times) may be suspended while a person reviews it. Keep-warm pinging is a breach of the acceptable-use policy.

10. Your content

You are responsible for what you generate and what you do with it. The models are made by third parties and published under their own licences; their makers are not party to these terms and have not endorsed this service.

  • Who the contracting entity is, and governing law and venue.
  • Warranty disclaimer, limitation of liability, indemnity.
  • Refunds beyond the ones stated above; chargebacks.
  • Suspension and termination procedure and notice.
  • Tax handling by country.
  • Whether the 12-month expiry on rounds is lawful everywhere we sell (prepaid-credit and gift-card rules differ by state and country).
  • Auto-renewal disclosures and cancellation flow (US state auto-renewal laws, ROSCA).
  • Payment processor acceptance of a dark-fiction text API under the acceptable-use policy, in writing, and a fallback processor.
  • The API's own privacy and logging terms, checked against the live gateway configuration.
  • Age assurance: whether a self-declaration is enough where we sell.
  • Changes-to-terms procedure and notice period.
