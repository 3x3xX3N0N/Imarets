Acceptable use

As of 2026-09-20. Short on purpose. It applies to every key, every pass, every tap.

verdantbloom.bar is a fiction-writing and developer API. It is not sold, described or promoted as an adult-content service.

1. Who

18 or older only.

2. Never

  • No sexual content involving minors, or involving anyone presented as a minor. Not in fiction, not in roleplay, not "aged up", not as a test.
  • No sexual content about real people.
  • Nothing illegal where you are, or where we are.

Keys that do this are closed without refund.

3. Yours

You are responsible for what you generate and for what you do with it. The models here will write dark fiction, horror and violence if asked; that is what they are for. What you publish, send or act on is on you.

4. The bar itself

  • Do not hold a tap warm without using it: no keep-alive pinging, no timed empty requests.
  • Do not try to read, probe or interfere with another member's traffic, or with the taps, the gateway or the status desk.
  • One key, one member. Do not resell or share a key.

Each pass has request limits (per minute, and at the same time). They are part of the pass, not a challenge.

5. What we do about it

We may suspend a key while we look into a suspected breach, and close it if the breach is real. A key closed for anything in section 2 is closed without refund. We report what the law requires us to report.

6. Telling us

If you see this service being used to break section 2, write to bloom@verdantbloom.bar.
