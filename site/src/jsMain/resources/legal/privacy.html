Privacy

As of 2026-09-20. This page covers this website: the landing page, the waitlist form and the reduced-fee application form. It says what those store and why. Nothing more is claimed.

It does not cover the API. What the API stores will be written down in its own policy. Until then, nothing is promised about it, here or anywhere on this site.

1. Who

verdantbloom.bar, a business. Contact: bloom@verdantbloom.bar.

2. The waitlist form

What we store when you submit it:

  • your email address;
  • which pass you said you were eyeing, if you picked one;
  • which form or page the sign-up came from;
  • when you signed up, and whether and when you clicked the confirmation link;
  • the single-use token behind that link.

Why: to send you one confirmation mail, and after you confirm, to write to you about the opening and the final prices. The pass you picked tells us which shelves people want. That is the whole use.

If you never click the confirmation link, you are not on the list and we do not write to you again.

3. The reduced-fee application form

What we store when you submit it:

  • the email or handle you gave;
  • the pass you asked for and the rate you said you can manage;
  • the sentence or two you wrote about why;
  • your country, if you gave one;
  • that you ticked the box saying it is true;
  • when it arrived, which fixes your place in the line.

Why: so a person can read it, decide it, and keep the waiting list in order. We ask for no documents and no proof. Please do not put anything in the free-text box that you would not want a person to read: it is stored as you typed it.

Nothing is charged by either form. There is no payment on this site.

4. What the server sees

Like any web server, ours sees your IP address when your browser asks it for something. The forms use it to limit how fast one address can submit. The forms also carry a hidden field that people leave empty and bots fill in. Form contents are not written to request logs.

5. What stays in your browser

The animated bloom reacts to you: pointer movement, scrolling, window size, whether the tab is visible, the timing of keystrokes in the email field (the rhythm only, never the letters), and device tilt if you switch it on. All of that is used on your screen and is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Two things do go out. The status sign and the pour list ask our server which taps exist and which are warm. The "ring for service" bell sends one request to the same place, and the page times the answer. Those requests carry nothing you typed.

Fonts and scripts are served from this site. The page loads nothing from third-party hosts, and it runs no analytics and no advertising.

6. Who else handles it

  • Confirmation mail is sent through SendGrid (Twilio), so SendGrid handles your email address in order to deliver it.
  • The site and its database run on servers we rent from a hosting provider.

We do not sell the list. We do not share it with anyone else.

7. How long, and getting off it

No fixed retention period has been set yet. Until one is, we keep a waitlist entry or an application until you ask us to remove it, or until the list has served its purpose and we delete it.

To be removed, to see what we hold about you, or to correct it, write to bloom@verdantbloom.bar from the address you signed up with, or tell us the handle you used.

8. Age

This site and the service behind it are for people 18 or older.

9. Changes

This page will change when the API gets its own policy. The date at the top changes with it.
