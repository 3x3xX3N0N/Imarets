The fine print

As of 2026-09-28. Everything on the prices page rests on these.

How a session is counted

  1. A writing hour is counted in 45-minute sessions: about 40 replies of about 400 tokens, each reply inside the minute.
  2. Context is billed every turn, so long contexts and thinking models drink faster. If you run 16k or more of context for hours a day, read the FAQ before you pick a pass.
  3. Pause longer than a minute between messages and the next pour is cold: a wait of about 30 seconds and the cold minimum. The hours above assume you do not.
  4. Rates per shelf are on the pour list. All prices are the whole price, tax excluded.

Disclaimers

  • Prices as of 2026-09-28. They are our best estimate of cost, not an introductory offer; they may move with notice.
  • We are not the cheapest way to run any of these models. Our list rates are set above Anthropic's published Claude prices (Sonnet 5, Opus 5, Fable 5.1 as of 2026-09-20) and we intend to keep them there.
  • A level is not unlimited. It is a monthly tab in dollars spent at the listed rates, plus request limits. When the tab is dry, buy another round or wait for the reset.
  • The free seat is one per person, limited to 10 requests a day (up to 20 with guests), 8k of context, 512 tokens a reply, one request at a time. Free seats on the same network share a daily cap. When the house tap is packed, members pour first and free requests may get a 429.
  • Unused tab does not roll over. Rounds you bought do, for 12 months from purchase; then they expire.
  • Context is billed as input on every request. There is no cached-input discount. Chat clients resend the whole history each turn, so long contexts burn the tab faster.
  • Every metered pour has a floor: the GPU time your reply took plus the warm time since your last message (up to a minute), at the card's per-second price plus 30%. The waiting part is shared between the people drinking from that tap in that minute. Waking a cold tap adds the wake-up time, paid by whoever woke it. Pours served by the always-warm house tap have no floor; if the house tap is packed and a member's pour is served by the serverless copy instead, the ordinary floor applies.
  • Cold pours take about 30 seconds (23-44 s measured; up to ~45 s on 48GB taps). Serverless taps scale to zero and go cold 60 s after the last request. Warm/cold status is shown per tap.
  • Reasoning tokens from thinking models are billed as output, including reasoning your client hides.
  • Session estimates assume about 400 output tokens per reply and thinking off unless stated. Never read a 6k-context figure without the 16k figure next to it.
  • Taps are shared. Each tap is one GPU running one model; several people are served by the same tap at once. That sharing is what lowers cost.
  • Reduced-fee seats and other below-list seats are capped at 25% of paying seats (floor of 3 while there are fewer than 12 payers). Reduced-fee prices are a separate application-based programme funded by patrons, not a sale.
  • A reduced-fee price is granted one month at a time. No click, no charge: the account drops to the free seat. A member's monthly price never rises without a click on a button that showed the amount.
  • The comparison with Anthropic's prices is a comparison of published list prices on the date shown and nothing more. We are not affiliated with Anthropic. Claude models are more capable than ours at most tasks; we make no claim about quality, capability or speed.
  • Unsubscribing from news email never affects a free seat, a code, a badge, house luck or a reduced-fee seat.
  • A round for the house is a payment to a business, not a charitable donation, and is not tax-deductible.
  • House luck is a free draw with no purchase, no entry and no claim cost; odds are equal for every eligible person; awards have no cash value. Void where prohibited.
  • Referral perks are described by mechanism, not amount, until prices are final. The figure is sent before you are ever asked to pay. After opening, the free-request bonus counts a guest once they have poured on three separate days. Opening credits need a level you paid for yourself, expire 90 days after opening and have no cash value.
  • Levels renew monthly until cancelled. Cancel any time; the level runs to the end of the paid period.
  • 18+ only. See acceptableUse. You are responsible for what you generate.

The stay-above rule

Every class stays at least 1.10x its Claude anchor on both input and output. Checked on the 1st of each month against Anthropic's published list. If Anthropic raises a price, we follow with 30 days notice. If Anthropic cuts, we do not have to. List rates are never discounted; cheaper arrives as a bigger tab, a reduced-fee price, or an unlisted plan.

Minimum pour

floor = cardRatePerSecond x 1.30 x ( busySeconds + idleSeconds / k [+ coldStartSeconds, never shared] ); charge for a request = max(token price of that request, floor)

The picture

Send an image to any tap. The bar reads the picture to the writer: the vision bottle (Qwen3-VL 8B, abliterated) describes it, the description is handed to the tap you chose, and that tap keeps writing in its own voice. Two pours: the description (Well-class rate, about 300 tokens out) and your own request. The description is billed to your key and tagged vision_bridge in the ledger.

Acceptable use

  • 18 or older only. This applies to the free seat exactly as it does to paid levels.
  • No sexual content involving minors or anyone presented as a minor. No sexual content about real people. Nothing illegal where you or we are. Keys that do this are closed without refund.
  • No sexual, degrading or deceptive content about real, identifiable people. No harassment, threats, fraud, malware, or help with weapons capable of mass harm. No passing off output as a real person's words.
  • The service is sold as a fiction-writing and developer API. It is not sold, described or promoted as an adult-content service. That is a statement about how we sell it, and it has to stay honest in both directions: marketing never leans on sexual content, and nobody (a customer, a processor, a host) is ever told the models cannot or will not produce explicit fiction, because they can.
  • Report abuse: abuse@verdantbloom.bar. Reports are read by a person. Keys that break this policy are closed.
  • Referral sharing: include the disclosure sentence, share where it is welcome, and make no claim we do not make ourselves (cheapest, unlimited, private). Codes are disabled on reported abuse.
