WARM / COLD

Why is my pour cold?

Because the bar is quiet. Every tap is its own GPU, and a GPU nobody is using gets switched off. Taps scale to zero when the bar is quiet, so a cold pour takes about 30 seconds. After that the tap stays warm for a minute past your last message. Reply inside the minute and it stays warm. Take longer and the next pour is cold again: another wait, and the cold minimum.

A cold tap means not enough people are drinking here yet.

While you read and type, the tap you woke is sitting warm and already paid for. The next drinker on that tap shares it, and rides on it for nearly nothing. Two drinkers on one tap: the pour costs us about half. Four: about a quarter. That is the whole trick, and it is the only one.

More drinkers, warmer taps, cheaper pours. For everyone.

So drink from the house pours. They are marked. They are the taps most likely to be warm. Every drinker who follows the crowd makes the pour cheaper for the crowd. LAST CALL is the schedule that hands it back.

Minimum pour.

Every pour holds a tap for a minute, so every pour costs at least a minute: 1.2 / 1.5 / 2.5 cents by shelf. Waking a cold tap costs about double: 2.5 / 3 / 5 cents. Reply within a minute with a normal-sized context and you will not notice either. Short prompts, or long pauses between messages, and you will.

Taps are shared. Each tap is one GPU running one model; a few members may be served by the same tap at the same time. That sharing is what lowers the cost.
